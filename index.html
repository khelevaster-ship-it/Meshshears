How it works

  1. Pick a label on the right, one for each piece you want to keep (shirt, belt, boots…). Remove marks what goes.
  2. Paint with the Brush, or click once with Fill to take a whole area up to its edges.
  3. Turn with a right drag, zoom with the wheel, slide with a middle drag.
  4. Switch on Preview the cut to see only what is kept, then Save. File exports the pieces as models.
